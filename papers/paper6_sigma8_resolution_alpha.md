Planck CMB and direct weak-lensing measurements (KiDS, DES, HSC)

naturally if dark matter is a fuzzy ultralight Bose field, as predicted

suppression of σ_8 at small scales via quantum pressure, matching the
Planck-vs-lensing tension at ~1% precision without parameter tuning.

The mechanism is independent of the QNG cosmological framework
otherwise reproducing ΛCDM at <2% accuracy across BAO redshifts.
Combined with an independent Lorentz Invariance Violation (LIV)
prediction (η_LV = 0.0116, testable by CTA), QNG offers two
quantitatively specific observational signatures distinct from ΛCDM.
