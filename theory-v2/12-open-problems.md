## Gap 5: Cosmological α value

**Problem**: in screened Poisson, `α = 0.005` is INPUT. Yet for cosmological
identification (`λ_screen ~ R_Hubble`), we need `α ~ 10⁻¹²⁴` in natural
units.

**What's known**:
- Dimensional argument: α has dimensions [length]⁻², suggests p=2 power-law
- This would give factor-15 match across 125 orders of magnitude
- BUT: classical α is L-independent (CPU-141 falsified the dimensional running)

**What's needed**: rigorous one-loop β-function calculation (DER-QNG-081
sketch). Estimated 5-8 weeks of analytical work.

**Status**: open. Without resolution, Λ_obs explanation remains conjectural.

## Gap 13: Scale separation Planck → MeV/GeV

**Problem**: substrate at Planck scale (a_M ~ 1.5 m_Planck per node), but

or non-Abelian gauge sector that runs to strong coupling.

**Status**: open. Blocks ALL quantitative particle physics predictions.

## Gap: Particle masses (electron, proton, muon, ...)

particle masses. QNG has no Higgs analog.

**Connected to Gap 13**: even if QNG had particle identification, the
22-order scale separation prevents quantitative mass predictions.

**Status**: open. Would require:
- Solution to Gap 13 (running mechanism)
- Plus: identification of QNG configuration → SM particle correspondence
- Plus: derivation of Yukawa-like coupling structure

## Honest scope statement

**QNG is a substrate theory for fundamental constants** (c, G, ℏ, Λ=0)
and gives a unified framework for **GR static-source phenomenology**.
With axiomatic v11 + v12 extensions, it accommodates linearized GR and
electromagnetism.

**QNG does NOT** derive particle masses, dark matter, non-linear GR,
or beyond-Standard-Model physics. These are open and may require:
- Multi-week to multi-year theoretical effort (Gap 13)
- New axiomatic extensions (DM, SU(2), SU(3))
- OR acceptance that QNG is INCOMPLETE — a substrate framework, not
  a complete theory of nature
