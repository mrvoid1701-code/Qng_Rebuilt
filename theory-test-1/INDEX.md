## FINDING (box-uniqueness) — the experiment's answer

**The QG box is NOT unique — it is a FAMILY.** Two independent discrete boxes, same
constraints C1–C7:
- **CONVERGE** on the skeleton: discrete/finite, Lorentzian geometry, a GR limit, quantum
  amplitudes, UV-finite → **the constraints are nearly forced**.
- **DIVERGE** on the flesh: **Λ** (QNG =0 + holographic V_0 vs causal-set ~10⁻¹²² nonzero),
  **Lorentz** (QNG emergent/η_LV vs causal-set exact), **background-independence** (causal
  set wins — even derives the dimension), **QM/matter depth** (QNG wins — Born rule, matter=|ψ|²).
- **Net:** the primitive you choose decides the specifics. Causal sets win on foundations;
  QNG wins on dynamics. See `tests/tt1_rung6_comparison_vs_qng.py`.
