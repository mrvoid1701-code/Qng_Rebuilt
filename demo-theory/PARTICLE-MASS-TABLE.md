## The structure (why it works)

- **Baryons = φ-solitons (Skyrmions):** mass scales LINEARLY (GMO in m); octet
  J=1/2, decuplet J=3/2 (collective quantization).
- **Mesons = φ-Goldstone bosons:** mass scales as m² (GMO in m²); the pion is the
  φ-quantum (Phase 5 triplet).
- The **m²-vs-m** distinction is the Goldstone-vs-soliton signature — QNG predicts
  both from one substrate (φ Goldstone + φ-soliton).
- The **overall scale** (proton ≈ 938 MeV) is dimensional transmutation of the
  confining edge-SU(3) coupling (Phase 11/12), set by α_s(M_P) ≈ 0.0153.

## Honest scope — read this

- The hadron **ratios and multiplet structure** are reproduced to ~1%. These are
  the **SU(3)-flavor / chiral (Eightfold-Way) relations** that QNG inherits as a
  **topological-soliton (Skyrme) + chiral** theory of hadrons — the *same*
  relations the quark model gives. They are genuine within the structure, but not
  unique-to-QNG predictions.
- The **absolute scale** (the ~940 MeV) is the **α_s input** (Drumul 3); making it
  parameter-free needs the gravity-induced fixed point (Phases 14–15) + the
  graviton dynamics (Phases 16–20).
- The **SU(3) breaking** (~strange mass, the ~147 MeV decuplet spacing) is a
  second input.
- **Leptons and quark masses are NOT here** — they need the v13/v14 matter sector
  (Yukawa couplings), genuinely open.
