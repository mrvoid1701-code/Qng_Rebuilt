## The picture

The QNG soliton-particle (φ-vortex / Skyrmion) is a genuine dynamical object with
the **full particle phenomenology**, all emerging from the substrate:
- it **annihilates** with its antiparticle into radiation;
- it has **radial (breathing) and rotational (J) excited states** — an internal
  spectrum;
- it carries **topological charge** (electric, baryon) that is conserved;
- it **binds** to other solitons via an attractive force (→ nuclei/molecules);
- it is **stable or unstable** by topology (proton-like vs resonance-like);
- it has a **mass** = its volume charge, at the dimensional-transmutation scale.

None of this is assumed — it is measured dynamically on the lattice.

## Honest scope

- Experiments use the 2D φ-vortex (baby-Skyrmion) and the collective-coordinate
  Skyrmion — tractable proxies for the full 3D SU(2) baryon, which would show the
  same qualitative behaviors with richer (pion-radiation, isospin) structure.
- Quantitative laws have caveats: the force law's precise form needs field
  relaxation (Phase 29); the breathing frequency's absolute value needs the
  collective kinetic mass + scale (Phase 28). The QUALITATIVE behaviors
  (annihilation, breathing, binding, stability) are robust.
- On a torus net topological charge must be 0, so annihilation (+1/−1) is the
  clean experiment; like-charge configs need compensating defects.
