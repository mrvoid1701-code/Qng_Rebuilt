## 1. How we got here

We started page `00` from the most primitive fact — a node has a phase, a phase
turns, frequency is fundamental. We followed it through synchronization (`01`),
the lightcone (`02`), and asked where light is (`03`). Two experiments answered:

- **E5:** a single scalar `φ` cannot be light. Its edge field `θ = ∇φ` is
  curl-free (transverse part = 0 to machine precision). One scalar = one
  longitudinal mode = **sound, not light**.
- **E7:** *no* node scalars can be light. For any node scalar, `curl(∇·) = 0`
  identically (Hodge `d∘d = 0`), so `χ`-circulation is zero and two coupled
  scalars still give only longitudinal modes. **But** a fundamental edge-vector
  field reproduces the photon exactly — 2 transverse polarizations at `c_φ`,
  longitudinal frozen.

The boundary between the two halves is **exactly the Hodge boundary**: exact
1-forms (`dφ`, gradients of node scalars) live in the longitudinal sector;
co-exact 1-forms (genuine edge circulation) live in the transverse sector. The
substrate cannot move a degree of freedom across that boundary by coupling — it
is geometry, not dynamics.

## 3. Why this rescues v12 instead of embarrassing it

Before this thread, v12's edge gauge field `A_ij` looked like a bolt-on: "we
wanted a photon, so we added one." E7 reframes it:

> Given that QNG's node fields are all scalars, **an edge gauge field is the

And it predicts the *next* door: the **graviton's two tensor polarizations**
(`h₊`, `h×`) cannot come from the node scalar `σ_g` (that gives only spin-0 —
this is exactly main-theory **Gap 12**). By the same logic they must be a
**rank-2 edge object**. E5/E7 turn Gap 12 from a puzzle into a prediction: look
on the edges, one rank higher than light.

The "missing piece" Gabriel sensed was real — it just was not a third theory.
It was the **edge sector**, and its first inhabitant is light.
