## Section 1 — What ℏ requires (structural analysis)

Planck's constant ℏ is not a single number. It is a **scale that appears
in multiple independent structural roles** in quantum mechanics. For ℏ
to emerge naturally from a substrate, the substrate must produce all of
these simultaneously.

### 1.1 Non-commutativity (algebraic role)

**Definition**: ℏ sets the scale of canonical non-commutativity:
```
[x̂, p̂] = iℏ I
```
Classical mechanics has `{x, p} = 1` (Poisson bracket, zero commutator).
Quantum mechanics has `[x̂, p̂] = iℏ` (non-vanishing commutator).

**Minimum requirement**: the substrate must have **operators** (not just
numbers) that satisfy non-trivial commutation relations. If a substrate
has only commuting real-valued observables at each node, it is algebraically
classical.

**Known mathematical structures that provide non-commutativity**:
- Heisenberg algebra (basic QM)
- Weyl commutation on discrete lattice: `U V = e^{iℏ} V U`
  where U = translation, V = multiplication
- Quantum group algebras (e.g., SU_q(2))
- Non-commutative geometry (Connes)
- Loop algebras (Loop Quantum Gravity)

### 1.2 Complex amplitudes (representation role)

**Definition**: quantum state is represented by a complex-valued wavefunction
`ψ(x) ∈ ℂ`, not a real field. Phase and amplitude are both physical.

**Physical consequence**: interference. Two classical amplitudes add as
reals; two quantum amplitudes add as complex numbers, producing
interference patterns.

**Minimum requirement**: the substrate must have **a complex-valued
degree of freedom** OR provide a mapping from real-valued states to
complex amplitudes consistent with interference.

### 1.3 Path integral measure (dynamical role)

**Definition**: quantum amplitude for transition between states is
```
⟨final|initial⟩ = ∫ Dq(t) exp(i S[q] / ℏ)
```
Sum over ALL trajectories with complex weight `exp(iS/ℏ)`. Classical
limit (ℏ → 0) picks out the stationary-phase trajectory (single classical
path).

**Minimum requirement**: the substrate must provide **many-histories
structure** with complex weighting. A substrate with unique deterministic
evolution from each initial condition has no path-integral structure.

### 1.4 Probability (interpretive role — Born rule)

**Definition**: `P(outcome x) = |ψ(x)|²`. Probability density is squared
modulus of complex amplitude.

**Minimum requirement**: substrate must produce **probability distributions**
from its state space, compatible with Born rule normalization.

### 1.5 Hilbert space structure (state-space role)

**Definition**: pure quantum states are unit vectors in a complex Hilbert
space `H`. Observables are Hermitian operators `Ô: H → H`. Inner product
`⟨ψ|φ⟩` gives overlap.

**Minimum requirement**: linear superposition principle. Substrate must
allow `ψ = αψ_1 + βψ_2` to itself be a valid state.

### 1.6 Unitary evolution (conservation role)

**Definition**: `ψ(t) = U(t) ψ(0)` with `U†U = I`. Preserves `‖ψ‖²`.
Generated by Hamiltonian: `U = exp(-iHt/ℏ)`.

**Minimum requirement**: evolution preserves the probability norm of
the state. Classical evolution preserves phase-space volume (Liouville),
which is a different conservation law.

### 1.7 Measurement postulate (observation role)

**Definition**: measurement collapses `ψ → |eigenstate⟩` with probability
given by Born rule. Classical measurement is a passive readout; quantum
measurement is dynamically non-trivial.

**Minimum requirement**: substrate must distinguish "dynamics without
observation" from "dynamics with observation." This is the hardest
requirement; most classical substrates fail here.

### 1.8 Discrete spectrum (quantization role)

**Definition**: bound states have quantized energy: `E_n = ℏω(n + 1/2)`
for harmonic oscillator. Angular momentum quantized: `L_z = ℏ m` with
integer m.

**Minimum requirement**: substrate must produce integer-labeled
eigenvalues from a continuous evolution. This is the most direct
"signature" of ℏ.

### Summary table — eight structural requirements for ℏ

| # | Role | Requirement | Classical analog |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Algebraic | [x̂, p̂] = iℏ | {x, p} = 1 |
| 2 | Representation | Complex wavefunction ψ | Real field |
| 3 | Dynamical | Path integral Σ exp(iS/ℏ) | Single trajectory |
| 4 | Interpretive | |ψ|² probability | Deterministic value |
| 5 | State space | Hilbert space superposition | Phase space point |
| 6 | Conservation | Unitary evolution | Liouville evolution |
| 7 | Observation | Measurement collapse | Passive readout |
| 8 | Spectral | Discrete eigenvalues | Continuous |

For ℏ to emerge with numerical value consistent with all eight roles,
the substrate must produce all of them from its own structure.

## Section 2 — What QNG v8 has (current inventory)

### 2.1 Node-state components

Each node `i ∈ V(G)` carries real-valued state:
```
(σ_g_i, σ_m_i, χ_i, φ_i, π_m_i, π_φ_i) ∈ ℝ⁶
```

with constraints: σ ∈ [0, 1], χ ∈ [-1, 1], φ ∈ [-π, π]. Only `(σ_m, π_m)`
and `(φ, π_φ)` are canonical pairs (have symplectic structure). `σ_g, χ`
are non-canonical (no conjugate momentum).

### 2.2 Graph structure

Fixed cubic lattice L³, z=6 neighbors per node. Adjacency `E(G)` is
static — no edge dynamics.

### 2.3 Hamiltonian H_v8

```
H_v8 = T_g[χ] + T_m[π_m] + T_φ[π_φ] + E_v7[σ_g, σ_m, φ] + V_couple[σ_m, φ]
```

where:
- T_g = (K_BACK/2) Σ χ² — kinetic-like (but χ is also dissipative)
- T_m = (1/2μ_m) Σ π_m² — standard kinetic
- T_φ = (1/2μ_φ) Σ π_φ² — standard kinetic
- E_v7 includes Channel A/B/D/F/G coupling energies
- V_couple = (g/2)(σ_m_ref - σ_m)²(1 - cos φ) — Yukawa φ-mass

### 2.4 Symplectic pairs (canonical brackets)

```
{σ_m_i, π_m_j} = δ_ij
{φ_i, π_φ_j} = δ_ij
{σ_g_i, ?} = 0  (no conjugate)
{χ_i, ?} = 0    (no conjugate; χ is dissipative in Channel D)
```

### 2.5 Evolution operator

Yoshida4 symplectic integrator. Time-reversible, preserves phase-space
volume, preserves H_v8 to <0.1% over 10000 lu. Deterministic: same
initial state → same trajectory always.

### 2.6 Conservation laws

- Energy: H_v8 conserved (symplectic)
- Translations: P_x, P_y, P_z conserved
- Topological charge: `M_ring = N·σ_ref - Σσ_m` conserved under Phase-3
  dynamics (no Channel A, F)
- Phase winding: `W_φ = ∮ dφ / 2π` is an integer invariant

### 2.7 Derived objects

- Effective metric `g_μν` (DER-QNG-044 Einstein correspondence)
- Effective Newtonian potential Φ ∝ δ_C (GRAV-C1)
- Emergent c_φ from `BETA_PHI/μ_φ` KG dispersion

## Section 3 — Gap analysis: what's missing

Compare requirements (Section 1) with inventory (Section 2):

### 3.1 Non-commutativity (Req 1): ABSENT

QNG Poisson brackets: `{σ_m, π_m} = 1` — classical. There is NO
non-zero commutator `[σ̂_m, π̂_m] = ?·I`. This is the single most
fundamental missing ingredient.

**What we forgot**: we have symplectic pairs but never DEFORMED them
to non-commutative operators. The step:
```
{A, B} = C   →   [Â, B̂] = iℏ Ĉ
```
is the **Dirac quantization** step. We never did it.

### 3.2 Complex amplitudes (Req 2): ABSENT

`φ ∈ [-π, π]` is a REAL angle, not a complex phase. The field `e^{iφ}`
that would be complex is computed implicitly in V_couple (through
`cos φ`), but `φ` itself is stored as a real number.

**What we forgot**: a complex field `Ψ_i = σ_m_i · e^{iφ_i}` would
unify σ_m (magnitude) and φ (phase) into a single complex amplitude.
We have the pieces but never merged them.

### 3.3 Path integral (Req 3): ABSENT

Yoshida4 integrator produces ONE trajectory per initial condition. There
is no sum over histories with complex weights.

**What we forgot**: we simulate dynamics on a **single history**; Feynman
path integral requires summing over MANY histories with weights
`exp(iS/ℏ)`. Monte Carlo path integral (Euclidean) is a standard
technique we have not implemented.

### 3.4 Probability / Born rule (Req 4): ABSENT

QNG state at time t is a definite real-valued configuration. No
probability distribution P(state) is naturally defined.

**What we forgot**: we never defined a **measure on the space of
configurations**. Statistical mechanics does this via Boltzmann weight;
QM does this via |ψ|². QNG has neither.

### 3.5 Hilbert space (Req 5): ABSENT

Phase space ℝ^(6N) is a vector space, but it is REAL. Hilbert space is
COMPLEX with an INNER PRODUCT `⟨ψ|φ⟩ = ∫ ψ*(x) φ(x) dx`.

**What we forgot**: we never defined a space of *states* as a separate
object from *configurations*. A state |ψ⟩ in QM is a probability
amplitude over configurations, not a configuration itself.

### 3.6 Unitarity (Req 6): PARTIAL

Yoshida4 preserves phase-space volume (Liouville theorem). This is a
WEAKER condition than unitarity. Unitarity is `U†U = I` on complex
Hilbert space; Liouville is `det(∂_q ∂_p) = 1` on real phase space.

**What we forgot**: Liouville ≠ unitary. These are related but not
equivalent. Unitary evolution implies Liouville, but Liouville does
not imply unitary (only symplectic).

### 3.7 Measurement (Req 7): ABSENT

No distinction between "simulation running alone" and "simulation being
observed". All observations are passive readouts of real-valued fields.

**What we forgot**: measurement postulate is the most controversial part
of QM (collapse / decoherence / MWI / QBism disputes). We have not
addressed it at all.

### 3.8 Discrete spectrum (Req 8): PARTIAL (topological only)

- Phase winding W_φ: **integer-quantized** (topological, yes)
- All other observables (H, L_z, M_ring): continuous spectra

**What we have that quantizes**: topology. That's a start.
**What we don't have**: energy spectrum quantization, angular momentum
quantization.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Non-commutativity [x̂,p̂]=iℏ | **MISSING** | Fundamental |
| 2 | Complex wavefunction | **MISSING** | Fundamental |
| 3 | Path integral Σexp(iS/ℏ) | **MISSING** | Fundamental |
| 4 | Born rule probability | **MISSING** | Fundamental |
| 5 | Hilbert space | **MISSING** | Fundamental |

**Score: QNG v8 has 2/8 partially, 6/8 completely missing.**

QNG v8 is **85% classical theory**. It has only topology (integer
winding) and symplectic structure (Liouville) in common with quantum
mechanics. Six of eight quantum requirements are structurally absent.

## Section 4 — Root causes: what did we forget?

### 4.1 Forgot deformation quantization

The step from classical `{A,B}` to quantum `[Â,B̂] = iℏ Ĉ` is
**deformation quantization** (Kontsevich, Moyal, Weyl). This is a
MATHEMATICAL PROCEDURE, not a physical mechanism. We never applied it
to our symplectic pairs `(σ_m, π_m)` and `(φ, π_φ)`.

Fix: define operators `σ̂_m`, `π̂_m` with commutator `[σ̂_m, π̂_m] = iℏ_lattice`.
Lattice version of canonical commutation.

### 4.2 Forgot that phase is fundamentally complex

φ stored as real number ∈ [-π, π], but physically phase IS complex
`e^{iφ} ∈ U(1)`. We used cosine in V_couple but did not make φ a proper
complex degree of freedom.

Fix: replace `φ` with `z = |z| e^{iφ}` where `|z|` could be σ_m. Then
`z` is a complex order parameter like in Ginzburg-Landau / BCS.

### 4.3 Forgot path integral is a thing

We have ONE evolution algorithm (Yoshida4) producing ONE history. We
never considered summing over multiple histories with weights.

Fix: implement Euclidean path integral (Monte Carlo sampling of
trajectories weighted by `exp(-S_E/ℏ)`). Then ℏ enters as a
parameter of the sampling weight.

### 4.4 Forgot that states ≠ configurations

QM has two levels:
- Configuration space: what classical values could the system have?
- State space (Hilbert): probability amplitudes OVER configuration space

QNG v8 collapses these two into one (the real-valued configuration IS
the state). We never built the second level.

Fix: define `ψ_i ∈ ℂ` as a complex amplitude over the configuration
`σ_m_i`. Then |ψ|² gives probability of observing that configuration.
This requires lifting to a larger (probabilistic) state space.

### 4.5 Forgot the name was "Quantum"

The most important self-criticism. We named the theory "Quantum Node
Gravity" but never added quantum structure beyond the classical
substrate. This was intellectual drift — starting with intent to
quantize, then getting absorbed by classical phenomenology.

**Re-commitment**: the "Q" in QNG should not be aspiration; it should
be STRUCTURAL. Every foundational object must support the eight
quantum requirements, at least in skeleton form.

## Section 5 — Correction roadmap

Rather than continuing to test emergent-ℏ mechanisms that fail (because
the substrate has no quantum structure to start with), we propose a
foundational rewrite that BUILDS IN the quantum requirements.

### 5.1 v10: Foundational quantum substrate

**Primary change**: replace real-valued node state with complex-valued:
```
v8:   (σ_g, σ_m, χ, φ, π_m, π_φ) ∈ ℝ⁶
v10:  (σ_g, Ψ, π_Ψ, χ, π_χ) where Ψ = σ_m · e^{iφ} ∈ ℂ
```

The complex field `Ψ` unifies amplitude and phase into a single object.
Standard Ginzburg-Landau order parameter structure. Provides Req 2
(complex amplitude) immediately.

### 5.2 Operator algebra with lattice commutator

Define lattice commutator:
```
[Ψ̂_i, Ψ̂†_j] = ℏ_lattice · δ_ij
```

This is equivalent to promoting (σ_m, φ) → annihilation/creation
operators `â_i = σ_m_i · e^{iφ_i}`. Standard bosonic lattice quantization.
Provides Req 1 (non-commutativity).

ℏ_lattice = some multiple of lattice spacing × ??? — must be derived
from action scale of substrate.

### 5.3 Path integral foundation

Evolution rewritten as:
```
⟨state_f | U(t) | state_i⟩ = ∫ DΨ exp(i S[Ψ] / ℏ)
```

Discrete time-slicing: each time step samples Ψ from a probability
distribution weighted by `exp(i dS / ℏ)`. In Euclidean version,
`exp(-dS_E / ℏ)` — proper Metropolis-Hastings works.

Provides Req 3 (path integral) and Req 4 (Born rule via |amplitude|²).

### 5.4 Unitary evolution

With complex Ψ, unitarity is natural:
```
Ψ(t+dt) = exp(-i Ĥ dt / ℏ) Ψ(t)
```

Crucially, `Ĥ` is now a Hermitian operator on the Hilbert space of
complex configurations, not a real-valued Hamiltonian function.

Provides Req 6 (unitarity).

### 5.5 Hilbert space structure

Formal state:
```
|Ψ⟩ = Σ_configurations c(config) |config⟩,   c(config) ∈ ℂ
```

Superposition is natural (just adding complex amplitudes). Provides
Req 5 (Hilbert space).

### 5.6 Discrete spectrum from topology + dynamics

Quantized spectra emerge from:
- Topology: W_φ ∈ ℤ (already have)
- Periodic boundary conditions: k_n = 2π n / L
- Commutation relations: H eigenvalues at integer multiples of characteristic scale

Combining these should give natural discrete spectrum. Provides Req 8.

### 5.7 Measurement — the hard one

Measurement in v10 could be defined as:
```
measurement = tracing over environmental degrees of freedom
           + decoherence suppresses off-diagonal terms of ρ(config, config')
           + remaining diagonal terms give Born probabilities
```

This is standard decoherence theory. Not a unique solution to measurement
problem (doesn't solve MWI vs QBist debate), but provides operational
Born rule. Req 7 partially addressed.

## Section 8 — Philosophical consequences

### 8.1 Why we arrive here only now

We started QNG with real-valued substrate because:
- Intuitive: classical fields are what everyone visualizes
- Computable: real Yoshida4 much simpler than complex path integral
- Traditional: most lattice models start real (Ising, XY, Heisenberg-classical)

We attempted to derive ℏ from this classical setup because:
- Aspirational: "quantum" is in the name
- Analytically possible: SED, Adler, 't Hooft, Smolin all claim classical→quantum emergence
- Ideological: avoid "just postulate ℏ" as intellectually unsatisfying

Result: **18-20 failures because the substrate is structurally unfit
for quantum behavior**.

### 8.2 What Einstein would say

Einstein-mind agent repeatedly noted: "ℏ is axiomatic; a theory honest
about its axioms is stronger than one pretending to derive them."

Einstein himself (1949 Autobiographical Notes):
> "I tried to discover the universal formal principle which could lead
> to certain results."

He meant: certain results (like ℏ's specific value) cannot be derived
without already having the **structure** that makes them possible.

In our case: we cannot derive ℏ without first having operators,
non-commutativity, and path integrals. Those are PREREQUISITES for
ℏ to have meaning.

### 8.3 What this means for the name "Quantum Node Gravity"

The name is **aspirational**, not yet structural. To make it structural:
- v10 must have intrinsic complex amplitudes (not real fields)
- v10 must have non-commutative operators (not just Poisson brackets)
- v10 must have path integrals (not just deterministic evolution)
- v10 must have Born rule (not just deterministic readouts)

Then "Quantum" becomes a structural property of the theory, not a
namesake.
