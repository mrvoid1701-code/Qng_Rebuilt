## Why DM is structurally impossible in current QNG

The QNG v12 charge is determined by phi-winding around any 2D loop
encircling vortex core. For TOPOLOGICAL stability (preventing dissolution
into vacuum), need π_1(target) = Z, which is exactly what gives charge.

**Topological stability ↔ Electric charge are LINKED in v12.**

A configuration that is BOTH:
- Topologically stable (resists dissolution)
- Electrically neutral (no charge)

requires either:
- Cancellation of charged components within bound state (unstable, like positronium)
- Higher-rank topology (Skyrmion, Hopfion-without-vortex) — not derivable from current fields
- A field beyond v12 (would require v13 extension)
