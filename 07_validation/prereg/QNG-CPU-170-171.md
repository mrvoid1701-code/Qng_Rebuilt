## Combined verdict

**THE REAL PROBLEM**: QNG framework has no well-defined notion of
equilibrium mass under current dynamics.

- Dissipative dynamics: doesn't converge in accessible time (>30000 lu)
- Conservative dynamics: exactly conserves arbitrary Phase 2 endpoint
- Neither gives intrinsic equilibrium

The session's 1-2% identification precision was an artefact of:
- Synchronized oscillation phases at PHASE3=3000 lu
- Arbitrary protocol choices (PHASE1=300, PHASE2=1500)
